Prototype gesture vocabulary

These gestures are demonstration gestures for the prototype and are not intended to represent a complete sign language.

  • ✋HELLOOpen palm, fingers spread
  • ✊HELPClosed fist
  • ☝️YESIndex finger raised
  • ✌️TWOIndex and middle raised
  • 👍YESThumb up
  • 👎NOThumb down
  • 🤏QUESTIONThumb and index extended, pointing

Coming next

This prototype only covers camera → landmarks → gesture → word. These are not built yet:

  • Sentence generation. Turning a sequence of gestures into a natural sentence, not just single words.
  • Two-way communication. Speech-to-text so a hearing person can reply in text.
  • Custom gestures. Letting a person record and label their own signs.
  • On-device privacy mode. Confirmation that no frames ever leave the browser.

SignSpeak is a functional prototype. Hand tracking runs locally in your browser — no video is uploaded anywhere.
